When does the DPD clock start?

Run two clocks in parallel — they answer different questions and shouldn't be conflated.

Internal
Starts at return date (Day 0). Drives charge-off triggers, bureau reporting at 30/60/90, merchant settlement reversals, and risk reporting. Don't restart this when retries fail — you'll hit charge-off later and roll deeper into uncollectible buckets.
Customer
"Past due" framing in messaging waits until retries are exhausted (around Day 10 for NSF, Day 7 for non-NSF). It's unfair to tell a customer they're past due while you're still automatically trying.
Why both
Aligns risk metrics with industry conventions while keeping the customer experience honest. Most best-in-class operators (utilities, telco, BNPL) decouple the two.

Operating principles

  • NACHA permits up to 2 re-presentments of an NSF return within 180 days of original settlement, but exact retry timing is dynamic — both attempts typically land within the week following the original return.
  • Always lead by reminding the customer who Trustly is and which merchant the payment was for — they may not be on a Trustly-branded surface.
  • Always disclose the bank-account hold: the original account is paused for future Trustly payments until the situation is resolved.
  • Self-serve repayment accepts bank, debit, and credit. Live agents enter the picture at Day 45.
  • Hardship and payment-plan options surface from Day 30; settlement offers from Day 75.
  • All messaging complies with Reg E, FDCPA, TCPA, and applicable state laws; STOP/opt-out language on every SMS and email.
